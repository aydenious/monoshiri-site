Privacy Policy

Monoshiri · Effective 9 November 2026

Monoshiri does not collect, store or share any personal data with its developer.

Your server

The documents, photos, web links and questions you add, your chats and your account are sent only to the Monoshiri server whose address you enter. That server's operator controls that data under their own policy.

AI models

Your server answers questions with the AI models its operator has configured. These can run on the server itself, or the server may send your documents and questions to a third-party AI provider. The app asks for your permission before it sends anything for the first time.

On your device

Your sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain. The server address, your last workspace and copies of documents you open are kept in the app's own storage and are removed when you delete the app.

Monoshiri reads only the files and photos you choose to add. It uses the camera only when you choose to take a photo of a page.

No tracking

Monoshiri contains no analytics, advertising, crash reporting services or third-party tracking.
