Monoshiri

The iPhone app for your self-hosted knowledge base.

Add your ebooks, papers, photos of pages and web links, then ask questions about them. Every answer shows the passages it came from.

Getting started

Monoshiri needs a Monoshiri server that you or someone you trust runs. Enter the server's address in the app, then sign in with your account on that server.

The server code is on GitHub at github.com/aydenious/monoshiri. It is based on SurfSense 2.1.0, and full credit goes to the SurfSense project.
